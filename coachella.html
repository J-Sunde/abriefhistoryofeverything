01
1993 – 1999

Born from a Fight

Before there was a festival, there was a lawsuit. Pearl Jam's war with Ticketmaster led a band to play a polo field — and changed American music forever.

1993

Pearl Jam vs. Ticketmaster

Pearl Jam refuses to route their summer tour through Ticketmaster, filing a formal antitrust complaint with the DOJ. The battle to play independent venues begins.

CatalystIndustry
1996

The Empire Polo Club Show

Pearl Jam plays the Empire Polo Club in Indio to a crowd of 25,000 as part of their Ticketmaster-free tour. The venue's potential is impossible to ignore.

Pearl JamIndio
1998

Goldenvoice Gets the Rights

Promoter Paul Tollett and Goldenvoice negotiate a deal with the Empire Polo Club. Tollett's vision: a multi-day, multi-stage festival unlike anything in America.

GoldenvoicePaul Tollett
1999

The First Coachella

October 9–10, 1999. Beck, Tool, Rage Against the Machine, and Morrissey headline. Tickets cost $50. Attendance is 25,000. The festival loses money but plants a flag.

DebutBeckTool
02
2001 – 2007

The Indie Underground

After a two-year hiatus and near-death, Coachella reinvented itself as the definitive showcase for independent and alternative music — before those words lost their meaning.

2001

Return from the Dead

After skipping 2000, Coachella returns as a one-day event. Jane's Addiction, Daft Punk, and The Chemical Brothers headline. The electronic stage is born.

Daft PunkRevival
2002

The Two-Day Experiment

Expands to two days with The White Stripes, Björk, and Oasis. The Sahara tent — Coachella's iconic electronic stage — becomes a full-scale destination of its own.

BjörkWhite Stripes
2004

The Radiohead Effect

Radiohead's Sunday night headliner is widely considered one of the greatest festival performances ever. The show cements Coachella as a pilgrimage, not just a concert.

RadioheadIconic
2007

Rage, Björk, and the Tipping Point

Rage Against the Machine reunite exclusively for Coachella. The event sells out in hours. Coachella is no longer a music festival — it's a cultural event with a guest list.

RATMReunion
“
Coachella is where careers are made and legends are confirmed.
— Industry consensus · Two decades of booking history
03
2008 – 2014

Going Mainstream

As the festival doubled in size with a second weekend, the booking philosophy shifted. Pop, hip-hop, and electronic music moved to center stage alongside indie rock.

2008

Madonna and the Pop Pivot

Madonna headlines — a shock to the indie faithful. It's the first sign that Coachella's identity is evolving beyond its alternative roots into something broader and more commercial.

MadonnaPop
2012

The Second Weekend

Coachella splits into two identical weekends for the first time. Capacity effectively doubles. The event becomes the highest-grossing music festival in North America overnight.

ExpansionBusiness
2012

The Tupac Hologram

Dr. Dre and Snoop Dogg's set features a 3D projection of Tupac Shakur performing. The moment goes globally viral and sparks a decade of conversation about digital resurrection.

TupacViralTech
2014

Arcade Fire's Moment

Arcade Fire's Sunday headline is the last great indie coronation at Coachella. After this, the booking balance permanently shifts toward pop, hip-hop, and electronic artists.

Arcade FireIndie
04
2015 – 2019

The Instagram Era

Coachella became the world's most-photographed event. The music mattered, but so did the flower crowns, the fashion shoots, the Instagram grid, and Beyoncé.

2015

The Fashion Festival

Fashion brands descend en masse. H&M, Revolve, and dozens of others host off-site parties. "Coachella style" becomes a searchable trend two weeks before the music even starts.

FashionBrand Activation
2017

Kendrick's Statement

Kendrick Lamar becomes the first hip-hop solo artist to headline. His performance is a dense, political, and artistic statement — widely considered one of the all-time great sets.

Hip-HopHistory
2018

Beychella

Beyoncé delivers a 2-hour HBCU-themed production that redefines what a festival performance can be. Netflix pays $60M to document it. The internet renames the whole festival.

BeyoncéIconicNetflix
2019

Ariana Grande Makes History

Ariana Grande becomes the youngest solo headliner in Coachella history. Her set draws some of the largest crowds ever recorded at the main stage in the festival's two-decade run.

ArianaRecord
05
2020 – Present

The Return

Coachella was cancelled for the first time in 2020 and 2021 due to the pandemic. When it returned in 2022, it reminded the world exactly why it had been missed.

2020–21

Cancelled

For the first time in its history, Coachella is cancelled — twice. The pandemic wipes out both editions. The silence of a missing April in Indio becomes its own cultural moment.

PandemicCancellation
2022

The Comeback

Coachella returns with The Weeknd, Billie Eilish, and Swedish House Mafia. Billie Eilish becomes the youngest solo headliner ever. The festival sells out in record time.

ReturnBillie Eilish
2023

Bad Bunny and the Latin Era

Bad Bunny becomes the first Latin artist to headline Coachella, performing almost entirely in Spanish. His set reshapes the conversation about who festival headliners can be.

Bad BunnyLatinHistoric
2025

Lady Gaga and the Spectacle

Lady Gaga ends a years-long live absence with a headline set that is part concert, part theatrical production. The return is covered as news, not just as a music event.

Lady GagaSpectacle
“
I want to create something that people will never forget.
— Beyoncé · Homecoming, Netflix · 2019
Culture & Aesthetics

The Coachella Look

Coachella didn't just change the music industry — it invented a dress code. The visual identity of the festival has shifted with each era, from dusty no-frills to full fashion-week deployment.

2001 – 2007

Indie & Effortless

Low-rise jeans, band tees, canvas sneakers. Nobody was dressing for the festival — they were dressing like they would for any show. The look was accidental.

Band Tees Denim No Filter
2008 – 2014

Bohemian Bloom

The flower crown era. Vanessa Hudgens and a wave of celebrity attendees turned the festival into a boho lookbook. Flowy dresses, crochet, fringe, and sun hats became the uniform.

Flower Crowns Boho Vanessa Hudgens
2015 – 2019

Brand Activation

Fashion brands arrive en masse. Revolve hosts its own off-site festival. Influencer culture fully absorbs Coachella. The outfit becomes the point — the music is the backdrop.

Revolve Influencers Brand Deals
2018 — Beychella

The HBCU Effect

Beyoncé's performance reframed the entire conversation. Greek letter apparel, Black collegiate fashion, and the visual language of HBCU culture briefly became the dominant aesthetic.

HBCU Beyoncé Cultural Reset
2020s

Desert Utility

Post-pandemic fashion leans into function: cargo silhouettes, technical fabrics, boots over heels. The maximalism remains but the reference points shift from boho to utilitarian-chic.

Cargo Utility Boots
Always

The Sun-Chic Constant

Across all eras, one truth holds: everyone is performing at Coachella. The audience is the show. Sunglasses, the golden hour, and the Ferris wheel have been the permanent backdrop since day one.

Golden Hour Ferris Wheel Desert Sun
The People Who Built It

10 Key Figures

Founder / Promoter
Paul Tollett

Goldenvoice CEO and the architect of Coachella. His vision of a multi-genre, arts-forward festival in the desert created the modern festival template.

Catalyst
Eddie Vedder

Pearl Jam's Ticketmaster fight directly led to the Empire Polo Club show in 1996 — the proof of concept that made Coachella possible.

Landmark Headliner
Beyoncé

Her 2018 "Beychella" performance is the single most discussed moment in Coachella history and redefined what a live event could mean culturally.

Electronic Pioneer
Daft Punk

Their 2006 and 2007 Sahara Tent sets established electronic music as the beating heart of Coachella and launched a generation of festival DJs.

Style Catalyst
Vanessa Hudgens

The unofficial style ambassador of the bohemian era. Her annual Coachella outfits were covered as fashion news and defined the flower-crown decade.

Hip-Hop Headliner
Kendrick Lamar

The first solo hip-hop headliner in 2017. His set was a political and artistic statement that permanently shifted the festival's center of gravity.

AEG / Business
Phil Anschutz

The billionaire owner of AEG, which acquired Goldenvoice in 2001 and provided the capital that allowed Coachella to survive its early losses and scale globally.

Art Director
Do LaB

The production collective responsible for Coachella's immersive art installations and stage environments that transformed the grounds into a walkable art world.

Latin Breakthrough
Bad Bunny

The first Latin artist to headline in 2023. His set — performed almost entirely in Spanish — expanded the definition of who a Coachella headliner can be.

Internet Era
Frank Ocean

His 2023 headline was the most anticipated in years — and his no-show at Weekend 2 was one of the most-discussed moments in festival history, online and off.
